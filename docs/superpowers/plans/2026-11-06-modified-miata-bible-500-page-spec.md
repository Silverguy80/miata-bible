# Modified Miata Bible 500-Page Master Manual Specification

**Goal:** Build a genuinely extensive, original, source-backed Miata repair and modification reference for NA, NB, NC, and ND owners.

## What “500 pages” means

The target is approximately 500 designed manual pages when rendered as a readable workshop publication, not 500 screens of padded prose. The content target is roughly 250,000–325,000 useful words plus original diagrams, tables, job sheets, symptom trees, wiring abstractions, fitment charts, and captioned reference imagery. The web app will expose the same material as searchable chapters, expandable procedures, print-ready job cards, and saved service queues.

## Copyright and authority boundary

Mazda factory manuals, Haynes, Bentley, Rod Grainger, Flyin’ Miata, ACT, EXEDY, and other references may be consulted for facts, terminology, fitment, and procedure verification. The app will not reproduce their wording, page scans, proprietary diagrams, connector pinouts, or tables. It will link to the source, explain what was independently synthesized, and mark exact specifications as requiring verification against the correct VIN, year, market, engine, transmission, trim, and modification state.

The manual will not claim to be a replacement for Mazda’s legal service information. Instead, its advantage will be the owner-facing layer Mazda’s service publications usually do not provide: symptom-first diagnosis, tool planning, access strategy, common failure patterns, decision points, mistakes, field tricks, modification side effects, buying logic, and a permanent build/service history.

## Evidence labels used throughout

**Factory-verified** means supported by an official Mazda service or owner source, an official supplier installation document, or a directly verifiable technical publication. **Supported practice** means a repeated, well-supported technique from reputable Miata specialists or manufacturers that still requires fitment judgment. **Field observation** means an enthusiast or shop pattern that is useful but not a universal specification. **Unknown / verify** means the information changes by variant or the evidence is insufficient; the manual will say so plainly.

## Publication architecture and page targets

### Volume 1 — Workshop system, diagnostics, and fitment: 60 pages

This volume teaches the reader how to identify the car, lift it, measure it, document it, and diagnose before buying parts. It includes safe support points by generation, fastener and tool strategy, seized-hardware workflow, electrical testing, voltage-drop diagnosis, compression and leak-down interpretation, cooling-system diagnosis, scan-tool discipline, service-history reconstruction, fluids and contamination, measurement forms, and an exact-fitment worksheet.

### Volume 2 — NA complete service and repair manual: 105 pages

This volume covers 1.6 and 1.8 engines, timing belt and water pump, front-cover reseal, cam and crank seals, oil leaks, cooling and fan circuits, intake and fuel delivery, ignition and no-start diagnosis, clutch and hydraulics, transmission and shifter turret, differential and axles, hubs and bearings, brakes, steering, suspension, pop-up lamps, charging and starting, rust diagnosis, soft top and drains, and common restoration traps. Each system gets removal, inspection, repair, reassembly, and verification procedures.

### Volume 3 — NB complete service and repair manual: 105 pages

This volume handles NB engine and fuel differences, variable trim and brake packages, Mazdaspeed-specific cautions, timing-belt and cooling work, intake and ignition diagnosis, clutch and lightweight-flywheel upgrades, gearbox and differential service, suspension geometry, ABS and lighting systems, frame-rail and rocker corrosion, soft-top and drain repairs, and common aftermarket wiring problems.

### Volume 4 — NC complete service and repair manual: 105 pages

This volume covers the 2.0 MZR engine, belt and cooling systems, clutch and flywheel replacement, six-speed and differential service, PPF and driveline alignment, suspension refresh, hubs and bearings, brake packages, ABS/DSC boundaries, PRHT diagnosis and drain service, body sealing, rust, electrical distribution, and post-repair scan or calibration decisions.

### Volume 5 — ND complete service and repair manual: 105 pages

This volume covers ND1/ND2/ND3 fitment boundaries, 1.5 and 2.0 engine differences, battery and low-voltage diagnosis, charging and network faults, clutch and flywheel work, gearbox and differential service, brakes and suspension, wheel bearings, RF roof interlocks, window coordination, body sealing, SRS and immobilizer boundaries, scan-tool workflow, and modification wiring that does not compromise CAN, SRS, or factory protections.

### Volume 6 — Modified Miata engineering, track setup, and build planning: 70 pages

This volume covers suspension selection, ride height, bump travel, damping, spring-rate logic, alignment, tire temperature reading, brake pad and fluid selection, heat management, oil cooling, forced induction preparation, fuel-system sizing, ECU and wideband wiring abstractions, intercooler and charge-air planning, clutch and differential limits, roll protection, seats and harnesses, track inspection, post-session diagnosis, street-versus-track tradeoffs, budgets, sponsor-safe product recommendations, and build-history templates.

**Minimum target: 550 pages, with no artificial maximum.** The extra margin keeps the manual above 500 pages after editing, layout changes, and removal of weak material. If a generation, system, trim, diagnostic pattern, or modification has useful verified information to add, it stays in the manual rather than being cut to protect a page count.

## Required depth for every major repair job

Every major job must include: what the symptom actually proves and does not prove; exact fitment questions; parts and buying logic; tools and access plan; safety gates; removal order; inspection measurements; decision points; repair or replacement choices; reassembly order; fastener and sealant cautions; hydraulic or electrical setup; initial test; road test; post-heat-cycle check; break-in; common mistakes; “stop and refer out” conditions; and source links.

A chapter that only says “inspect brakes,” “replace the clutch,” or “check wiring” fails review. The reader must be able to understand the sequence and the reasons behind it without the manual pretending that one generic torque number applies to every Miata.

## Original visual system

The manual will include a high volume of integrated visuals: original functional wiring maps, exploded-sequence illustrations that do not reproduce factory drawings, access maps, torque-pattern diagrams, suspension geometry sketches, symptom decision trees, inspection-point photos where licensing permits, captioned generation photos, and printable measurement sheets. Every image gets a descriptive caption and a source or provenance note.

## Product recommendation system

Each upgrade chapter will include a recommendation card with product category, supported generations, use case, why it is recommended, tradeoffs, value alternative, OEM-style alternative, required companion parts, and a direct product link. Links will be editorial until sponsorship or affiliate arrangements exist. Sponsored or affiliate placements will be visibly labeled and never allowed to override fitment or safety warnings.

## Build sequence

The manual will be built in reviewed tranches: first the shared workshop/diagnostics volume plus NA/NB drivetrain and engine work; second NC/ND drivetrain, cooling, chassis, and electrical work; third body, roof, rust, and generation-specific repair systems; fourth track, forced induction, product recommendations, diagrams, job sheets, and final editorial QA.

The app will show tranche progress honestly. It will never claim “500 pages” until the page-counted publication build, source ledger, procedure coverage checks, and print/export pass all confirm the target.
