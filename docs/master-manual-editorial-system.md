# Modified Miata Bible — Canonical Master Manual System

## Editorial promise

The app is one original, source-aware Miata reference. It synthesizes the coverage that owners expect from factory service information, commercial DIY books, enthusiast workshop references, motorsport setup notes, parts documentation, and field practice without copying their prose, diagrams, page layouts, or proprietary illustrations.

The manual has no artificial page ceiling. It grows until the useful coverage is complete, while every section earns its place through a repair task, diagnostic decision, generation difference, modification tradeoff, or reusable worksheet.

## The anti-duplication rule

A technical idea is written in full only once. Every later appearance uses a short cross-reference, a fitment delta, a warning, or a decision link.

For example, the canonical clutch-removal procedure owns the full workflow. The NA, NB, NC, and ND sections do not repeat the workflow. They contain only the generation-specific differences: access, fasteners to verify, wiring or sensors that change, special tools, known failure points, and links back to the canonical procedure.

The same rule applies to cooling diagnosis, brake bleeding, battery-drain testing, alignment, wheel installation, wiring safety, forced-induction planning, and every other repeated system.

## Content layers

### Layer 1 — Canonical task

One original procedure explains the reasoning, preparation, safe sequence, measurements, verification, common mistakes, and stop conditions.

### Layer 2 — Fitment overlay

A generation or trim overlay contains only what changes for NA, NB, NC, ND, engine, transmission, market, body, brake package, roof type, or option package.

### Layer 3 — Symptom index

A symptom entry points to the canonical diagnostic tree and lists the fastest first checks. It does not repeat the repair procedure.

### Layer 4 — Modification decision record

A build section explains goal, tradeoffs, required companions, fitment hazards, budget, and validation. It links to repair procedures instead of rewriting them.

### Layer 5 — Worksheet and evidence

Inspection forms, measurements, photos, torque records, scan data, alignment results, and build-history entries capture what happened on a specific car.

## Every technical entry must include

- Exact problem or task
- Applicable generations and exclusions
- Symptom or decision trigger
- Safety boundary and stop conditions
- Tools and measurement method
- Original step sequence
- Expected evidence at each decision point
- Verification test
- Common wrong turns
- Generation overlays or a statement that none are known
- Related canonical procedures
- Source notes and uncertainty label

## Evidence labels

- **Established:** supported by authoritative service information, manufacturer documentation, or repeatable measurement.
- **Strong practice:** widely used workshop practice with clear mechanical reasoning, but not a universal factory requirement.
- **Conditional:** depends on generation, market, trim, parts combination, climate, or prior modification.
- **Unknown / verify:** insufficient evidence or variation too large to state safely; the app must direct the owner to verify the VIN-specific source or measure the car.

## Source and copyright boundary

Sources inform coverage, terminology, and verification. They are not rewritten into disguised copies. The app uses independently authored explanations, original decision trees, original abstract circuit maps, original tables, and original captions. Exact torque values, connector pinouts, safety-system procedures, and VIN-specific limits remain verification points for authorized service information.

## Definition of done

The manual is not “complete” because it reaches 500 pages. It is complete only when the coverage matrix has no unexplained gaps, each repeated task resolves to one canonical procedure, each generation difference is captured as an overlay, every major symptom has a diagnostic path, and every safety-critical claim has an appropriate verification boundary.
