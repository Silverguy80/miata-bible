# Wheel & Tire Fitment Lab — source notes

These sources informed the fitment ranges in the app. The app intentionally presents them as measured starting ranges, not guarantees.

- [949 Racing wheel list](https://949racing.com/wheel-list/): published wheel dimensions, offsets, bolt patterns, load ratings, and application examples. Relevant entries include 15x7 +32, 15x7.5 +38, 15x8 +34, 15x9 +32 in 4x100 and ND-oriented 17x8 +38 / 17x9 +45 entries.
- [ThreePiece NC fitment guide](https://www.threepiece.us/blog/guide-to-perfect-fitment-on-your-nc-miata/): NC 5x114.3, 66.1 mm hub, M12x1.5, 80–90 ft-lb lug guidance; examples include 16x8 +38 with 205/50R16, 17x8 +45 with 215/45R17, 17x8 +48 with 225/45R17, and context on rear-lip rolling and tire width.
- [RimList NB wheel guide](https://rimlist.com/guides/mazda/miata/nb/wheels): NB 4x100, 54.1 mm center bore, M12x1.5, 86 ft-lb lug reference; factory configurations including 14x6 +45, 15x6 +40, 16x6.5 +40, and Mazdaspeed 17x7 +40.
- [Konig NA/NB fit check](https://konigwheels.com/blogs/fit-check/here-s-what-wheels-fit-your-mx-5-miata-na-nb-fit-check): practical NA/NB guidance around 7–7.5 inch width, ET20–40, and 15x8 ET25 as an aggressive example.

Important interpretation: actual clearance changes with tire brand section width, ride height, spring/perch design, alignment, brake package, bushings, chassis damage, fender condition, wheel face, and full-lock/full-bump geometry. Widebody entries in the app are planning windows and require mock-up against the exact kit revision.
