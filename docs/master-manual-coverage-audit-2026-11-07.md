# Modified Miata Bible — repair coverage audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** In progress — not yet a complete replacement for factory or commercial service manuals

## Bottom line

Modified Miata Bible currently contains a substantial original foundation, but it does **not** yet contain every repair operation represented across the factory service references, Haynes coverage, enthusiast workshop material, wiring references, and motorsport notes reviewed during planning. The current app should be described as a source-aware workbench and expanding master-manual project, not as a finished substitute for VIN-specific service information.

The correct target is not to copy or disguise any referenced manual. The target is a canonical, independently written task library with generation overlays, diagnostic routes, evidence requirements, safety boundaries, product guidance, and cross-links that cover the same practical repair territory without reproducing protected prose, diagrams, page layouts, pinouts, or proprietary illustrations.

## Current inventory

- 14-book manual architecture
- 54 canonical repair/service procedure records currently indexed by title
- 9 symptom-oriented diagnostic trees
- NA/NB/NC/ND generation research overlays and original functional circuit maps
- 18 performance-upgrade catalog records
- Repair-product recommendations attached across the current procedure layer
- Garage profile, build planner, installed-parts ledger, service history, and state-aware next-step queue

The book page numbers in the app are editorial scope targets. They are not a verified page count and must not be presented as proof of completed coverage.

## Coverage that exists today

The current canonical layer includes meaningful coverage for:

- Safe lifting, fitment records, baseline inspection, fluids, cooling triage, no-start diagnosis, parasitic draw, compression planning, clutch hydraulics, brake inspection, alignment baseline, wheel torque, soft-top drains, rust triage, charging, lighting, and track preparation.
- NA/NB/NC/ND clutch and lightweight-flywheel workflows.
- Performance baseline, suspension tuning, track brake thermal planning, and forced-induction planning.
- Symptom routes for no-crank, crank/no-start, rough idle, overheating, charging faults, parasitic drain, brake/steering complaints, NVH, water leaks, and ABS/DSC warnings.
- Timing-belt replacement planning for NA/NB and a timing-chain service boundary for NC/ND.
- Spark plugs, valve clearance, oil pressure, fuel pressure, intake smoke testing, water pump/thermostat, HVAC diagnosis, transmission fluid, differential fluid, hubs/bearings, brake pads/rotors, brake bleeding, ABS/DSC, scan workflow, aftermarket wiring, pre-purchase inspection, storage, and post-modification validation.

That is useful coverage, but it is not exhaustive repair coverage.

## What “complete” will mean

The manual should not be declared complete until each major system has:

1. One canonical task or repair procedure.
2. A symptom-first diagnostic route where a complaint commonly precedes the repair.
3. NA, NB, NC, and ND fitment overlays where the job differs.
4. Engine, transmission, market, trim, body, roof, brake, or option exclusions where relevant.
5. Safe tools, stop conditions, measurement method, and verification test.
6. Product and tool choices with OEM, value, and enthusiast alternatives where sensible.
7. Source notes and an explicit facts / misconception / verify boundary.
8. An original diagram, photo, worksheet, or text-equivalent visual for jobs where it materially reduces error.
9. Cross-links that prevent duplicate full explanations.
10. A publication audit showing no unexplained gaps in the coverage matrix.

## Publication boundary

No commercial manual should be copied, lightly paraphrased, or repackaged. The Modified Miata Bible must remain an original synthesis. Exact torque values, connector pinouts, wiring colors, calibration routines, SRS procedures, and VIN-specific limits should be treated as verification points against authorized service information rather than invented from memory.

Until the gaps above are addressed and audited, the responsible product language is: **“an expanding original Miata repair and build reference”**, not “the only manual anyone will ever need.”
