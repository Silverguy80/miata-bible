# Modified Miata Bible — repair coverage audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-23 (updated)  
**Status:** Canonical task matrix filled for the planned repair territory — still not a substitute for VIN-specific factory service information

## Bottom line

Modified Miata Bible now carries a full original canonical procedure library across the 14-book plan, including the prior foundation plus the ultimate-repair expansion (engine sealing and bottom-end planning, fuel/ignition/emissions sensors, cooling proof tests, automatic and LSD/Torsen driveline coverage, ABS hydraulic boundaries, setup windows, body water paths, grounding/EMI, and Tips & Tricks entries). Generation field manuals remain in `generation-content.js` / `docs/research/`.

The correct target remains original synthesis: one canonical task per job, generation overlays where needed, symptom routes, safety boundaries, and verification — without copying Mazda, Haynes, Chilton, or other commercial manuals.

## Remaining publication work (not missing repair topics)

These are quality and product tasks, not absent systems:

1. Deepen thin early stubs toward the clutch-job standard where a job is safety-critical.
2. Add more original diagrams for jobs where a figure reduces error.
3. Keep generation overlays thin; avoid duplicating full explanations.
4. Re-run a duplicate-title audit when editing large batches.
5. Preserve the copyright boundary: no proprietary prose, pinouts, or copied diagrams.

## Publication boundary

Exact torque values, connector pinouts, wiring colors, calibration routines, SRS procedures, and VIN-specific limits remain verification points against authorized service information.

Responsible product language: **an original, expanding Miata repair, tips, and build reference with complete canonical coverage of the planned repair matrix** — still not “the only document anyone will ever need instead of factory service information.”
